WELCOME
"Your employees represent more than themselves. They represent your company."
One of the top challenges in both private and public sectors is employee retention and development. Change and growth is the inevitable. During this time, the viability of the company is hinged upon talent development and management. Employees should be fully equipped to perform according to your standards during times of transition.
Forward Movement Enterprises, we help you to identify talent transformation opportunities by conducting a diagnostic assessment. Our instructional design team develops the most value-added curriculum customized to meet your specific needs. We deliver engaging training solutions proven to directly impact individual performance, team efficiency and business advancement. The transformation takes place at the point of our delivery.
Forward Movement Enterprises is a professional development training company based in the Baltimore Washington region. We deliver professional development solutions to private and public sector clients through instructor-led and virtual training platforms across the nation.
Instructor-led Training
Forward Movement Enterprises delivers a multi-mission curriculum, which incorporates a critical thinking shift that enhances initiative, quality, responsibility and achievement. We also pattern our training to highlight real world challenges coupled with solutions to promote forward movement. The uniqueness of our clients needs serve as our compass to deliver best-in-class support. We are fully committed to the success of our clients.
Virtual Training
This method of short module instruction referred to as microlearning supports knowledge retention. With heavy work schedules and leading teams that spread across the states this web-based instruction appeals to the visual, kinesthetic and auditory learner. Your company will receive logistic reduction, time savings and employees will benefit from an impactful knowledge transfer through high engagement, collaboration and the most memorable experience.
Dynamic Training
Engaged employees are happy and perform at a higher altitude. Our team takes pride in developing customized dynamic training that offers a pleasant interruption in standard training. We recommend this variation of training twice annually. Forward Movement Enterprises will help you increase employee engagement, responsibility and initiative with this out of the box dynamic shift in learning.
